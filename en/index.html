        01What do I need to open a laser tag business?

        Start with the format, venue and number of players. A kit includes player equipment, sensors and game control tools. The result depends on how you plan to operate. Describe your project, and we'll prepare a proposal.

        02Can I play without a permanent arena?

        Mobile and outdoor formats are possible. Equipment, a suitable space, transport and an operating plan must work together. Consider the mobile format when putting your kit together.

        03Can I expand equipment I've already bought?

        There are different generations of equipment and devices. Tell the team your models and platform: we'll check compatibility before you choose new equipment.

        04How are delivery and price calculated?

        The offer depends on configuration, quantity, destination and order terms. Request a quote for your project rather than relying on a general “from” price.

        05What are the warranty and support?

        A 2-year warranty; terms depend on the product, buyer type and contract. See the current policy and confirm the terms in your order.
